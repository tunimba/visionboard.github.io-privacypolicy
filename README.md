Vizea ("Vizea", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to Vizea and its various versions and is part of our Terms of Use. **Your photos never leave your device.** Vizea creates vision board images from the goals you select as text. It does not upload, transmit, or process your personal photos.

## 2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of data, none of which is linked to your identity:

### User Content

**Selected goals** — Used for App Functionality

When you create a vision board, the goals you pick (for example "travel more" or "start my own business") are sent as text to our AI provider so that a generic, stock style photograph can be generated for each one. Only this short scene description is sent. No photo, no name, and no device identifier accompanies it.

**Photos or Videos** — Not collected

Photos you take with the camera or add from your photo library are placed directly on your board and stored **only on your device**, inside the app's own storage. They are never uploaded to our servers or to any third party, and they are never sent to the AI provider. Deleting a board or the app removes them.

### Identifiers

**Device ID** — Used for Analytics, and Other Purposes

An anonymous device identifier is collected to ensure secure and reliable AI service routing. This identifier is not linked to your personal identity.

### Purchases

**Purchase History** — Used for App Functionality, and Analytics

If you make in-app purchases, transaction data is processed by Apple and RevenueCat to manage your subscription. This data is used to provide access to premium features and to analyze purchasing trends in aggregate.

### Usage Data

**Product Interaction** — Used for Analytics

We collect anonymous data about how you interact with the app (e.g., screens viewed, features used) through Firebase Analytics. This helps us understand how the app is used and identify areas for improvement.

### Diagnostics

**Crash Data** — Used for App Functionality

Crash reports are collected through Firebase Crashlytics to help us identify and fix issues that affect app stability.

**Other Diagnostic Data** — Used for App Functionality, and Analytics

Additional diagnostic information (e.g., performance metrics, error logs) may be collected to monitor app health and improve reliability.

### Other Data

**Other Data Types** — Used for Other Purposes, and Analytics

Anonymous service data, including IP address, is collected to ensure secure and safe AI service routing.

## 3. Face Data Collection, Use, and Retention

This section describes in detail how Vizea handles face data in compliance with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines (5.1.1).

### What face data does the app collect?

Vizea does **not** collect, store, transmit, or retain any face data. The app never uploads photographs of any kind. Photos you add to a board stay on your device; they are not sent to us, to our AI provider, or to any other third party. No facial features, biometric identifiers, or face geometry data are extracted, analyzed, or recorded at any point.

### How is face data used?

It is not used at all, because it is never collected. The images that Vizea generates are created from a short text description of the goal you selected, not from any photograph of you. The generated photographs are generic stock style scenes and are not based on, derived from, or matched to any person's likeness. The app does not perform facial recognition, face mapping, identity analysis, or any form of biometric identification.

### Is face data shared with third parties?

No. Since no photographs and no face data are collected, none can be shared. The only content sent to our AI provider is the short English scene description belonging to the goal you selected.

### How long is face data retained?

Face data is **not retained**, by us or by anyone else, because it is never collected. Photos you add to a board are retained on your own device only, for as long as you keep the board.

## 4. How We Process Your Goals

- **Text only:** When you generate a board photo, only the short scene description of the goal you selected is sent to our third-party AI provider, OpenRouter, through AIProxy.
- **Your photos stay put:** Photos you capture or pick are written to your device's app storage and drawn onto the board locally. They are never uploaded.
- **No storage of generated content:** Generated photographs are returned to your device and saved with your board on your device. We do not keep copies on our servers.
- **No biometric analysis:** The app does not extract or analyze facial features for identification or profiling purposes. No biometric identifiers are created, inferred, or retained.
- **No human review:** All processing is handled by automated AI models. No employee accesses or views your boards unless you explicitly contact our support team for help.
- **Consent first:** Before the first generation request, the app asks for your explicit consent to send your selected goals to the AI provider.

## 5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

- **OpenRouter** — AI image generation from the text description of your selected goal. It receives no photographs and no personal identifiers.
- **AIProxy** — Secure AI service routing. Uses an anonymous stable device identifier and collects IP address for security purposes.
- **RevenueCat** — Subscription and purchase management. Collects device identifiers and purchase history to manage entitlements.
- **Firebase Analytics** — Anonymous usage analytics. Collects device identifiers and product interaction data.
- **Firebase Crashlytics** — Crash reporting and diagnostics. Collects crash data and diagnostic information.
- **Firebase Remote Config** — App configuration delivery. No user data collected.
- **Apple In-App Purchases** — Secure transaction handling.

These services process data only to the extent required for their core functions. None of the data collected is linked to your personal identity.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Your photos:** Stored on your device only, for as long as you keep the board. Never uploaded, so nothing to retain elsewhere.
- **Selected goals:** Sent as text for generation and not stored by us afterwards.
- **Face data:** Not retained. No face data is collected at any point.
- **Analytics and diagnostics:** Retained in anonymous, aggregated form by third-party providers according to their own retention policies.
- **Purchase information:** Managed by Apple and RevenueCat under their respective privacy and retention policies.
- **No user data is linked to personal identity.**

## 7. Data Security

- Photos you add to a board never leave your device.

- We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data. While no system can guarantee 100% security, we continuously work to safeguard your information.
